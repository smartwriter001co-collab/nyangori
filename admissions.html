Admissions

All are welcome at Nyang'ori TVTC. We welcome learners from every background who are ready to build practical skills and shape their future.

Admission requirements

Our TVET CDACC training starts at Level 3. You are welcome to apply even if you left school after primary school or did not complete secondary school.

Bring your interest in learning and willingness to participate in practical training. Get in touch with us to discuss your chosen course and the enrollment process.

How to apply online

Fill in your basic information below. Your email app will open with your application addressed to the director. Review it and press Send to complete your application.

If your email app does not open, email your details to pagktvc2025@gmail.com.

Not sure where to begin? We will be glad to guide you.
